Top recommendation

Give the feed a clock seam

It's the README's own stated first priority, it changes zero external interface, and the fake-timer suite already pins the module at exactly the seam the refactor must preserve: the rare refactor whose safety net is fully in place before you start. Deepening domain.ts is the better story (it turns the seconds-not-percent argument into tested code) but touches two feature modules; right second move, not the pre-walkthrough one.
